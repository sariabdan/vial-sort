# Results

## Scope

This record owns what came out of the arm tests: where every run's counts and score live and how they are computed, the readings of them that the project draws, the comparison the home page draws, the written accounts of each model on the robot, and what is not solved. It does not own how a rollout is run or scored, the score's definition, the 12-move gate or the caveats; those are in [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md). What each model is belongs to [Models](../models/models.md), the training runs and offline probes to [Training](../training/training.md), and the dated session write-ups to the [Lab notes](../../experiments/experiments.md).

## Where the results live

The numbers of every run are in `src/data/results.json`, and the live [Results page](https://cognitive-software-labs.github.io/vial-sort/results/) renders them. This record does not copy them: a copy goes stale with every rebuild of the data. Read them in the data or on the page.

The JSON under `src/data/` is generated by scripts (`scripts/build_data.py`, `scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`) from the operator's notes in `notes/` and from rollout reports and recordings in a separate `~/lerobot` checkout that is not in this repository. Every count was measured on the arm (as scored); every score, share and verdict is a computation from those counts under the definition in `src/lib/score.ts`.

| What | Where it is |
|---|---|
| One row per run: model, checkpoint, mode, test days, rollouts, the three stages, restarts | `rows` in `src/data/results.json` |
| Total of scored rollouts | `total` in `src/data/results.json` |
| In the asked slot / rollouts, per destination slot and the bin | `by_destination` of each row |
| Picked up the right vial / rollouts, per source slot | `by_source` of each row |
| Where every vial ended, per slot asked for; runs scored from notes only | `confusion` of each row |
| In the asked slot as seen by a person | `seen_in_slot` of the row's `released`: for runs scored from notes the note is the check; for runs scored from the arm, the rollouts an eye check saw in the slot |
| Rollouts on moves no demonstration contains | `untrained` of each row; null when none was recorded |
| Rollouts left out, and why | `left_out` in `src/data/results.json` and per model in `src/data/experiments.json` |
| Every session and take, scored or not | `src/data/rollouts.json` |
| Score, trained-slot and held-out shares, verdict | Computed at build time by `scoreRun` in `src/lib/score.ts`; not stored in the data |
| Weak slots | `weakSlots` in `src/lib/score.ts` |
| Order of the run list | The sort in `src/pages/results.astro`: last test day, newest first, then rollouts, more first; grouped by model, newest model first |
| The written account of each model on the robot | The "On the robot" notes in `src/data/experiments.json`, from `models.yaml`; the Results page's "What happened" tab |

There is no script in this repository that prints the scores outside the site build. The website's own build is the reproduction: `npm run build`, then the Results page in `dist/`.

## Readings at commit 8065309

These readings were counted from the data at commit 8065309 (2026-11-28). They rerun nothing and change no conclusion. When the data is rebuilt, check them again before quoting them as current.

- **No run passed.** The data held 22 runs and 287 scored rollouts. Every run had fewer than 10 rollouts at every one of the 12 rack slots (the most at one slot was 9), so every verdict was "Disqualified: under 10 rollouts at 12 of 12 slots". The Results page still shows each run's numbers, over the slots it was tried at.
- **Held-out destinations were rarely tried.** Only five runs had a rollout asked for a held-out destination. The bin is never part of a score.
- **Isaac 0.5 Expert's far slots.** Of the 20 rollouts asked for slot 4 or 5, 15 ended at slot 3 and none at slot 5 (`confusion`).
- **Plain Bagel's right rack.** The right-rack held-out slots pull toward the middle: asked R2, it went to R1 three times; asked R4, to R3 four times of five (`confusion`).
- **Rollouts not in any run.** `src/data/rollouts.json` held 61 sessions and 335 takes; 287 were scored, 44 left out (restarts of noted moves, rollouts that ended within 3 s, commands the scorer cannot read, the earlier table layout of 7 Oct), and the 4 takes of Isaac's first session, 25 Nov, before the noted sheet, belong to no run (`SESSION_RUN` in `scripts/build_data.py`).

## What the home page compares: π0.5 on the 100 demonstrations versus the 220

Chapter 03 of the home page (`src/pages/index.astro`), "With the new data, the destination follows the command", draws three π0.5 runs, each the row with the most rollouts of its model: Mango Rice · 12k · sync (trained on the 100 demonstrations), Mango Pudding · 16k · sync (continued from Mango Rice, on the 220) and Plain Bagel · 16k · sync (trained from the base model on the 220). The Results page offers the same three as the preset "π0.5: 100 → 220 demonstrations" (`src/pages/results.astro`). Their numbers are those rows of `src/data/results.json`.

Chapter 02 says why the 220 were recorded: the first 100 demonstrations were recorded one destination per sitting, so the first model took the destination from the camera images; in the newer demonstrations one layout is recorded three times, to three destinations, so only the command can tell them apart ([Demonstrations](../demonstrations/demonstrations.md), [Training](../training/training.md)).

The paired comparison of the Lab notes is the cleanest one in the repository: Plain Bagel and Mango Pudding ran the same 31 moves on 8–9 Nov. Plain Bagel put 21 in the asked slot, Mango Pudding 11 (`src/data/experiments.json`, Plain Bagel's "On the robot" note; `docs/experiments/2026-11-09-a16000-arm-test/a16000-arm-test.md`). The Lab note ranks A better on 16 moves, B on 5, the same on 10, and records grasp failures 1 against 7. The 12-move gate outcomes of both are in [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md).

The home page's "In short" band describes the run with the latest test day; at commit 8065309 that was Isaac 0.5 Expert · 21k · sync. The [website record](../website/website.md) says how the band picks its run and its numbers.

## What each model did on the robot, as written

The written accounts are the "On the robot" notes in `src/data/experiments.json`, generated from `models.yaml`, and the Results page's "What happened" tab shows them. Where they disagree with the counts, that is listed under [Where sources disagree](#where-sources-disagree).

The Lab notes of 4, 8 and 9 Nov add per-move tables in the operator's words for Mango Lassi, Mango Sorbet, Mango Chutney, Mango Pudding and Plain Bagel ([Lab notes](../../experiments/experiments.md)). Their main findings, as written: on 4 Nov the grasp failures came from a normalisation mismatch (the gripper's range widened from 37.4° to 50.8° when the datasets merged), fixed by pinning the quantiles, after which the arm went to the middle of the rack whatever the digit; on 8 Nov the destination followed the command but the far slots were squeezed toward the middle; on 9 Nov Plain Bagel reached slot 6 in 9 of 9 and slots 4 and 5 once each, and what was left was mostly the drop.

## What is not solved

The home page lists it under "What is not solved" (`src/pages/index.astro`), with its numbers filled from the data at build time:

- **Unseen destinations.** The models do much worse on the slots no demonstration used as a destination than on the others.
- **The pass mark.** No run has reached 70% with at least 10 rollouts at every slot.
- **Small numbers.** Many runs have a handful of rollouts, so one rollout moves a run a long way.
- **Not a planned evaluation.** These are test runs made during development; see [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md).

The Lab note of 9 Nov names what was left for Plain Bagel then: mostly the drop (6 missed drops at the right slot, opening within 3–5° per joint of the successful releases), and the bumped left_top camera, whose effect is not proven.

## Where sources disagree

- **Rollout counts of 4 Nov.** The Lab note counts 35 rollouts for B at 4000 (6 full successes, 14 failed at the grasp), 10 for `004000_q37` (9 picked up, 1 placed) and 4 for `004000_q37g` (0 grasped). The data, scored from the arm, has Mango Lassi 37 rollouts (21 picked up, 6 in the asked slot), Mango Sorbet 11 (9, 1) and Mango Chutney 7 (1, 1) (`src/data/results.json`). The difference was not traced; the Lab note scores from the operator's words, the data from the scorer.
- **Mango Rice in sync mode.** Its account says two of six rollouts in the asked slot, both slot 3. The data has two sync runs: 12k with 1 of 6 (at L3) and 10k with 1 of 2 (at R3), so 2 of 8 together. It also says it took the right vial every time the command was worded exactly as in training; the data has 4 of 6 and 2 of 2, and does not record the wording.
- **Crispy Falafel at 30k.** Its account says it grasped the asked vial in 4 of 4 grasps; the data has 2 of 4 rollouts picking up the right vial (`crispy-falafel-30000-sync`). Whether "grasps" counts something other than rollouts is not stated.
- **Isaac at slot 4.** Its account says the vial went to slot 4 five times; the matrix has slot 4 three times, plus one dropped behind R4 (#23) and one wrong vial put in R4 (#4, counted as never picked up) (`notes/I_21000.txt`).
- **What counts as reaching the slot.** The Lab notes' 12-move gate counts a missed drop at the right slot as the right slot; the data counts it as not in the asked slot (see [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md)).
- **Pretzel Two's "On the robot" note** is shown under its model, but the table-top test it describes is "separate" from the scored rollouts and has no counts in the data.
