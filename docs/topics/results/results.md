# Results

## Scope

This record owns what came out of the arm tests: every run's counts and score as recorded in the data, slot by slot, the comparison the home page draws, the written accounts of each model on the robot, and what is not solved. It does not own how a rollout is run or scored, the score's definition, the 12-move gate or the caveats; those are in [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md). What each model is belongs to [Models](../models/models.md), the training runs and offline probes to [Training](../training/training.md), and the dated session write-ups to the [Lab notes](../../experiments/experiments.md).

## A dated snapshot

This record is a snapshot of the data at commit 8065309 (2026-11-28). The live [Results page](https://cognitive-software-labs.github.io/vial-sort/results/) is regenerated from the data on every rebuild and is the current view; when the two differ, the page is newer.

The JSON under `src/data/` is generated by scripts (`scripts/build_data.py`, `scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`) from the operator's notes in `notes/` and from rollout reports and recordings in a separate `~/lerobot` checkout that is not in this repository. The numbers here are as recorded in that data; none was re-derived from the recordings. Every count was measured on the arm (as scored); every score, share and verdict is a computation from those counts under the definition in `src/lib/score.ts`.

## How the tables were produced

- Counts (rollouts, picked up the right vial, in the asked slot, stages, days, restarts, per-slot counts, where every vial ended) are read from `rows` in `src/data/results.json`.
- Score, trained-slot and held-out shares, slots tried, verdict and weak slots were computed by a one-off Node script, outside the repository, that reads `src/data/results.json`, imports nothing from the repository and re-implements `scoreRun`, `weakSlots` and the rounding of `pctOf` from `src/lib/score.ts` line for line: per rack slot `released / rollouts`, the plain mean over the slots tried (bin left out), means over slots 1, 3, 6 and 2, 4, 5 of both racks, disqualified with fewer than 10 rollouts at any of the 12 rack slots, otherwise passed at 70%.
- Order: the Results page's run list (`src/pages/results.astro`): runs sorted by their last test day, newest first, then by rollouts, more first; then grouped by model, newest model first, its newest run leading its group.
- Check against the site's own numbers: the script gives Isaac 0.5 Expert 61% (trained 92%, held-out 30%) and Plain Bagel 47%, the figures `docs/topics/website/website.md` quotes from the home page's summary band (92% and 30%) and that the Results page renders from the same function. Unrounded: Isaac 60.83%, Plain Bagel 46.61%.

## Where sources disagree

- **Rollout counts of 4 Nov.** The Lab note counts 35 rollouts for B at 4000 (6 full successes, 14 failed at the grasp), 10 for `004000_q37` (9 picked up, 1 placed) and 4 for `004000_q37g` (0 grasped). The data, scored from the arm, has Mango Lassi 37 rollouts (21 picked up, 6 in the asked slot), Mango Sorbet 11 (9, 1) and Mango Chutney 7 (1, 1) (`src/data/results.json`). The difference was not traced; the Lab note scores from the operator's words, the data from the scorer.
- **Mango Rice in sync mode.** Its account says two of six rollouts in the asked slot, both slot 3. The data has two sync runs: 12k with 1 of 6 (at L3) and 10k with 1 of 2 (at R3), so 2 of 8 together. It also says it took the right vial every time the command was worded exactly as in training; the data has 4 of 6 and 2 of 2, and does not record the wording.
- **Crispy Falafel at 30k.** Its account says it grasped the asked vial in 4 of 4 grasps; the data has 2 of 4 rollouts picking up the right vial (`crispy-falafel-30000-sync`). Whether "grasps" counts something other than rollouts is not stated.
- **Isaac at slot 4.** Its account says the vial went to slot 4 five times; the matrix has slot 4 three times, plus one dropped behind R4 (#23) and one wrong vial put in R4 (#4, counted as never picked up) (`notes/I_21000.txt`).
- **What counts as reaching the slot.** The Lab notes' 12-move gate counts a missed drop at the right slot as the right slot; the data counts it as not in the asked slot (see [Evaluation](../evaluation/evaluation.md)).
- **Pretzel Two's "On the robot" note** is shown under its model, but the table-top test it describes is "separate" from the scored rollouts and has no counts in the data.
