# Evaluation

## Scope

This record owns how a model is tested on the arm and how it is scored: how a rollout is run, how the operator's notes are written and read, the outcome stages, the score of a run, the 12-move gate of the November arm tests, and what the numbers cannot say. It does not own the numbers that came out of the tests; those are in [Results](../results/results.md). What each model is belongs to [Models](../models/models.md), the offline probes to [Training](../training/training.md), the demonstrations and their held-out slots to [Demonstrations](../demonstrations/demonstrations.md), the arm, racks and cameras to [Task and rig](../task-and-rig/task-and-rig.md), and the dated session write-ups to the [Lab notes](../../experiments/experiments.md). How the website renders all of this is in the [website record](../website/website.md).

## Terms

The record uses the website's glossary (`src/lib/glossary.ts`) word for word where it defines a term.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Rollout | One autonomous attempt by the robot at one command, from the moment it is started until it stops or the session ends. |
| Session | One launch of the rollout program. A session holds several rollouts, all with the same checkpoint, the same mode and the same lighting. |
| Run | One model at one checkpoint in one mode (`src/lib/score.ts`, `src/pages/method.astro`). A row of the Results page. EMA weights are a separate run (`crispy-falafel-30000-sync-ema` in `src/data/results.json`). |
| Checkpoint | The model's weights saved at one training step; "12k" is the checkpoint after 12,000 steps. Results are never pooled across checkpoints. |
| Command | The sentence typed to the robot. It names a source and a destination by position, never by colour. |
| Move | One source slot and one destination, written R3 → L6 (in the data `R3->L6`). A destination can also be the bin. |
| Take | One recorded episode of a session (`<session>_t<n>` in `src/data/rollouts.json`). A noted move can have several takes; the last is the rollout, the earlier ones are restarts (`scripts/build_data.py`). |
| Scorer | A script (`assess_rollouts`) that reads the arm's joint angles during a rollout, finds each grasp and release, and works out which slot the arm was at by matching its pose to the same moment in the demonstrations. It is accurate to about half a slot and cannot see the vial. |
| Eye check | A person watching the cameras to see where the vial actually ended. Done for only some of the rollouts scored from the arm. |
| Held out | Never recorded on purpose: slots 2 and 5 are never a source, and slots 2, 4 and 5 never a destination. |
| Sync mode | The robot pauses while the model computes its next chunk of actions, then runs the chunk. |
| RTC | Real-time chunking: the model computes the next chunk while the arm is still running the last one. π0.5 supports it; FLUX 3 Action does not. |
| Total accuracy | The score of a run (defined below). "Score" in this record means total accuracy. |
| Trained-slot accuracy, held-out accuracy | The same mean over slots 1, 3, 6 and over slots 2, 4, 5 of both racks. |
| Weak slot | A rack slot where half or fewer of the rollouts asked to end there ended in the asked slot. |

## How a rollout is run

A rollout is one autonomous attempt at one command. The robot is the follower arm of the SO-101 pair on the workstation, with the five cameras of the Data page, and nobody drives it (`src/pages/method.astro`).

| Step | What happens (`src/pages/method.astro`) |
|---|---|
| Home pose | The arm is moved to the pose every demonstration started from, so a crash in the previous session cannot shift "home". |
| The command | The command is the sentence the model trained on. From 21 October a short code ("R3 L1") expands to the exact training wording; before that it was typed by hand. |
| The run | A person starts the rollout. The model reads the camera images and the joint angles, aims for a control rate of 30 per second, and sends joint targets to the arm. A person stops it, or it ends when a camera drops off the bus. |
| Replanning | The model replans every few dozen actions. In sync mode the arm waits for each replan; with RTC it does not. |
| Stored | Each session is stored as a LeRobot dataset with the cameras the model saw, one episode per rollout, and uploaded to Hugging Face. |

Who gives the command: the operator at the robot. The Lab notes show the short code typed as `/t R3 L1` and an optional `/note <what happened>` so the scorer can read the session back (`docs/experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md`, "Test protocol").

### Resets, restarts and retries

- The arm starts every rollout from the home pose (`src/pages/method.astro`).
- A restart is a new take of the same move. The operator's notes count one rollout per noted move: its result goes on the move's last take, and the takes before it are restarts, left out of the counts and counted as such (`scripts/build_data.py`, comments above `NOTE_STAGE` and in `october`). The notes guide writes a restart that was not the model's fault as `<result> (restart: <why>)` (`notes/*.txt`, "HOW TO WRITE A LINE").
- A retry the note mentions is written `<first try>, then <final result>`; only the part after the last "then" is scored, and the yaml marks the move `retried: true` (`scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`). So a move that needed a second try counts as its final result.
- Restarts in the data: 3 for Isaac 0.5 Expert, 11 for Plain Bagel, 6 for Mango Pudding, 20 in all (`restarts` per row and `left_out.restart` in `src/data/results.json`).
- In the 9 Nov session three moves were restarted after 8–13 s without a note, and the first try of L3 → R3 ran 39 s and never let go; the note says "success", so the scored result is the second try (`docs/experiments/2026-11-09-a16000-arm-test/a16000-arm-test.md`).
- Rollouts that end in under 3 s are left out of arm scoring as ended before the policy moved (`SHORT_TAKE_S = 3` in `scripts/build_data.py`).
- Rollouts 8 and 9 of 4 Nov were lost to a USB hub dropping the wrist camera, the right camera and both arms; the Lab note calls them not valid data (`docs/experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md`). A robot bus error stopped the first session of 8 Nov once (`docs/experiments/2026-11-08-b16000-arm-test/b16000-arm-test.md`).

## How a rollout is scored

A rollout is scored one of two ways, and a run is scored only one way: `scripts/build_data.py` stops if one run mixes rollouts scored from notes and from the arm.

| Scored from | Runs | How "in the asked slot" is decided |
|---|---|---|
| The operator's notes | Isaac 0.5 Expert, Plain Bagel, Mango Pudding (`noted_models` in `src/data/results.json`) | What the operator saw at the robot |
| The arm | All other 19 runs | The scorer: the arm letting go at the asked slot, within half a slot, unless a person saw the vial was not in it |

### From the arm: the scorer

The scorer works from the arm, not the vial, in four steps (`src/pages/method.astro`):

1. Read the arm's joint angles. It cannot see the vial.
2. Find each moment the gripper closed on something and each moment it opened.
3. Decide which slot the arm was at by matching its pose to the same moment of the demonstrations.
4. Put the rollout in the furthest of the three outcome stages it reached.

In `scripts/build_data.py` (`ws_stage`) a take whose source and destination both check out is "In the asked slot"; a take with the right source that was lifted is "Picked up, not in the asked slot"; anything else, including closing on the right vial without lifting it, is "Never picked it up".

### Eye checks

Because the scorer reads the arm and not the vial, a person also watched 53 of the rollouts scored from the arm: the vial was in the slot in 11, not in it in 39, and could not be told in 3 (`eye` in `src/data/results.json`). A rollout the arm scored as in the asked slot that a person saw outside it becomes "Picked up, not in the asked slot" (`scripts/build_data.py`, `rollouts`). Rollouts scored from the operator's notes are not counted as eye checks: there the note is the score. The checks are recorded in `curation.yaml` (`checked`), per the comment in `scripts/build_data.py`.

### From the operator's notes

#### The notes format

The operator types one line per move at the robot into `notes/<name>.txt` (`scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`, header; the guide at the foot of `notes/I_21000.txt`):

```
<n>: <source> <destination>: <what happened>
```

- A slot is a side and a number: L = left rack, R = right rack, 1–6 with 1 nearest the arm, or `bin`. Left and right are seen from the follower arm (the left rack is the top rack in the top camera).
- The side is written with the number (R3, not "slot 3"); the one exception is "stuck between slot 3 and 4".
- Lines that do not start like a move (a header such as `A 16000: plain bagel`, or `#` comments) are skipped. A line that starts with a number and a colon but does not read as a move stops the script.
- A number used twice gets a "b" the second time (25, 25b).

The writing guide, as printed in each notes file:

| What happened | Write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ended in the asked slot | `success` | `1: L1 R2: success` |
| Ended in another slot | `put in <slot>` | `3: L1 R4: put in R3` |
| Fell in front of or behind a slot | `missed drop in front of <slot>` or `dropped behind <slot>` | `13: L3 R4: missed drop in front of R3` |
| Never picked the vial up | `didn't grasp` | `8: R1 L3: didn't grasp` |
| Picked up another vial | `wrong vial (took <slot>), put in <slot>` | `4: L1 R5: wrong vial (took L3), put in R4` |
| Held it and never let go | `never let go` | `9: L3 R6: never let go` |
| Stuck between two slots | `stuck between slot <a> and <b>` | `9: L3 R5: stuck between slot 3 and 4` |
| Needed a second try | `<first try>, then <final result>` | `5: L1 R2: didn't grasp, then success` |
| Restarted, not the model's fault | `<result> (restart: <why>)` | `6: R1 L2: success (restart: vial knocked over)` |

|---|---|---|---|
| `notes/B_16000.txt` | Mango Pudding (B at 16000) | 31, 8–9 Nov | Free wording, before the guide (e.g. "missed placing ( a bit in front of the rack") |
| `notes/A_16000.txt` | Plain Bagel (A at 16000) | 82: the same 31 moves on 9 Nov, then blocks 32–82 on 24 Nov | Free wording for 1–30; the 24 Nov blocks closer to the guide |

#### From a note to an outcome: `scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`

The script reads only the part of a note after the last "then" (the final result) and applies these rules in this order; the first that matches decides:

| Order | The final part contains | Outcome | Where the vial ended |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | didn't / couldn't / did not / could not / won't (+ one word) grasp, grab, grip or pick; "missed (the) pickup / pick up / grasp"; "no grasp" | `no_grasp` | source |
| 2 | "wrong vial" | `wrong_vial` | source |
| 3 | "grabbed / grabed / picked / took / grasped (up) <slot>" naming another slot than the asked source | `wrong_vial` | source |
| 4 | "success" / "succeed" (after removing any "grabbed <slot>" phrase) | `placed` | the asked destination |
| 5 | "stuck", "never let go", "didn't / did not let go" | `never_let_go` | the two slots of "between <a> … <b>" on the destination's rack, else unknown |
| 6 | "in fron(t)", "behind", "missed plac(ing)", "missed (the) drop", "dropped" | `drop_missed` if the slot named ends up the asked one, else `off_slot` | the slot named after the drop word, else anywhere in the final part, else the last slot named before it, else the asked slot |
| 7 | A slot: "slot N", "Nth slot" (on the destination's rack) or "R2" as written | `placed` if it is the asked slot, else `off_slot` | that slot |
| 8 | "same as" and nothing above | the previous note's outcome when the move is the same; `no_grasp` or `wrong_vial` carried over when the previous note was one | as the previous note |

A note that no rule reads stops the script ("so nothing is guessed"); the operator rewords it or writes the yaml entry by hand. A note is marked `retried` when it says "at first", "retry/retried", "ran it twice" or "second try", or when a grasp failure or wrong vial appears before the last "then".

The script then gives every recorded take of the model's sessions (from `src/data/rollouts.json`, in session and take order) to a note of the same move. A take stays on the current note while the move is the same, moves on to the next note with another move when that one matches, and may go back one or two notes when a pair of moves was run twice. Notes in a row with the same move share their takes one each, the extras on the last. A note with no take, or a take that matches no nearby note, stops the script.

It writes `notes/<name>.yaml` beside the notes, one entry per noted move with `n`, `move`, `outcome`, `ended`, `retried`, `note` and `takes`. The yaml is not overwritten without `--force`, so hand fixes in it survive; `scripts/build_data.py` scores the sessions from the yaml.

#### The outcome kinds and how they map to the stages

| Outcome (yaml) | Meaning (`scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`) | Stage (`NOTE_STAGE` in `scripts/build_data.py`) | Column of the confusion matrix | Kind shown on a rollout video card |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `placed` | the vial ended in the asked slot | In the asked slot | the asked slot | Success: in the asked slot |
| `drop_missed` | let go at the asked slot, the vial ended beside it (in front, behind, on the rack) | Picked up, not in the asked slot | Dropped beside | Missed drop: beside the asked slot |
| `off_slot` | let go at another slot | Picked up, not in the asked slot | the slot it ended at | Wrong slot: put in <slot> |
| `never_let_go` | carried the vial and never let go | Picked up, not in the asked slot | Never let go | Never let go |
| `no_grasp` | never picked the asked vial up | Never picked it up | Not picked up | Failed pickup: never picked it up |
| `wrong_vial` | picked up another vial | Never picked it up | Not picked up | Failed pickup: took another vial |

The kind labels are in `clip_kind` in `scripts/build_data.py`; for rollouts scored from the arm the same function gives "Success: in the asked slot", "Never let go", "Missed drop: beside the asked slot" (when a person saw the vial outside the slot the arm let go at), "Wrong slot: let go at <slot>" and "Failed pickup: never picked it up". The column names are in `src/pages/results.astro`.

A vial dropped in front of or behind the asked slot is a failure, like one put in another slot. The comment in `scripts/build_data.py` calls this "the operator's rule, 24 Nov". A note marked `wrong_vial` counts as "Never picked it up" even when the other vial was then put in the asked slot, as in Plain Bagel's #40 "picked R6 and put in L2" (`notes/A_16000.yaml`).

### The three outcome stages

Every scored rollout is in exactly one stage, worst to best (`STAGE_DEF` in `scripts/build_data.py`, `stages` in `src/data/results.json`):

| Stage (key) | Definition |
|---|---|
| Never picked it up (`no_grasp`) | The arm never picked up the vial in the asked source slot: the gripper closed on another vial, on an empty hole or not at all, or closed on the right vial but never lifted it. |
| Picked up, not in the asked slot (`carried`) | The arm picked up the right vial, but it did not end in the asked slot: let go at another slot, dropped in front of or behind the asked slot, or never let go before the rollout ended. |
| In the asked slot (`released`) | The vial ended in the asked slot. With the operator's notes, that is what the operator saw. Scored from the arm, it is the arm letting go at the asked slot (within half a slot), unless a person saw the vial was not in it. |

The data keys stay `released` and `carried` "so older data and code keep working" (`scripts/build_data.py`); the words on the site are the labels above. A run's funnel is Rollouts → Picked up the right vial (`grasped_right`, every stage but `no_grasp`) → In the asked slot (`released`) (`funnel` in `src/data/results.json`).

The Results page draws each stage share as a vial filled in one of four steps (`src/components/SlotVials.astro`, `src/pages/method.astro`):

| Step | When | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| all | every rollout got through | teal |
| most | half or more | lighter teal |
| some | under half | orange |
| none | none | empty vial, count in red |

A slot never tried is a faint vial; a dashed vial is a slot the run's training set never used in that role (held out). "Picked up from" counts, per source slot, the rollouts that picked up the right vial; "Placed into" counts, per destination, the rollouts that ended in the asked slot.

### Where every vial ended: the confusion matrix

For runs scored from the operator's notes, `scripts/build_data.py` (`confusion_of`) builds a table with a row per slot asked for and a column per place the vial ended: each of the 12 rack slots and the bin, "Dropped beside", "Never let go" and "Not picked up". Its diagonal is the score's "in the asked slot", and the script stops if the matrix and the row's per-destination counts disagree. Runs scored from the arm have no matrix: the arm cannot see where the vial ended. The Results page adds recall per row and precision per column (`src/pages/results.astro`):

- Recall of slot s: the rollouts asked for s that ended in s, over all rollouts asked for s. This is the share the score uses.
- Precision of slot s: of the vials that ended at s, the share that were asked to end there. A false positive is a vial that ended at s while another slot was asked. Precision is not part of the score.

## The score of a run

Defined once in `src/lib/score.ts` (`scoreRun`), "as agreed with the supervisor on 24 Nov 2026" (comment at the top of the file), and explained under "The score of a run" on the Testing page. This is a definition, not a measurement.

### Definition

`scoreRun` receives a run's `by_destination` counts: for each destination, the number of rollouts asked to end there and the number that ended in the asked slot (`released`). It returns:

| Field | Computed as |
|---|---|
| `total` (total accuracy, "the score") | For each of the 12 rack slots L1–L6, R1–R6 that has at least one rollout as the destination, the share `released / rollouts`; then the plain mean of those shares. The bin is left out. |
| `trained` (trained-slot accuracy) | The same mean over L1, L3, L6, R1, R3, R6, the destinations the demonstrations used |
| `held` (held-out accuracy) | The same mean over L2, L4, L5, R2, R4, R5, which no demonstration used as a destination |
| `tried` | Rack slots with at least one rollout |
| `short` | Rack slots with fewer than 10 rollouts (an untried slot counts as 0) |
| `verdict` | `disqualified` if `short` > 0; else `passed` if `total` ≥ 0.70; else `below` |

A slot with no rollout is left out of the mean, so a disqualified run's numbers are "still shown, over the slots it was tried at". A mean over no slot is null and is shown as "–". Percentages are rounded to whole numbers (`pctOf`: `Math.round(x * 100)`).

| Constant (`src/lib/score.ts`) | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `RACK_SLOTS` | L1–L6, R1–R6 | The 12 slots the score averages over |
| `HELD_OUT_SLOTS` | slots 2, 4, 5 of both racks | Held-out destinations |
| `TRAINED_SLOTS` | slots 1, 3, 6 of both racks | Trained destinations |
| `MIN_PER_SLOT` | 10 | Minimum rollouts at every rack slot to pass |
| `PASS_MARK` | 0.7 | Minimum total accuracy to pass |
| `WEAK_AT` | 0.5 | A slot at or below this share is weak |

In formula form, with S the 12 rack slots, n_s the rollouts asked for s and TP_s those that ended in s:

- recall_s = TP_s / n_s
- total accuracy = mean of recall_s over the slots of S with n_s ≥ 1 (the Testing page writes it as (1/12) Σ recall_s, which is the same when all 12 were tried)
- pass ⇔ n_s ≥ 10 for every s in S and total ≥ 70%
- weak(s) ⇔ recall_s ≤ 50%

Verdict wording on the site (`verdictText`): "Passed", "Below 70%", or "Disqualified: under 10 rollouts at k of 12 slots".

Weak slots (`weakSlots`): the tried slots at half or less in the asked slot, worst share first and, on equal shares, the slot with more rollouts first, written "R2 0/3". Slots never tried are not listed.

The Results page opens on the run with the highest score, and among equals the one with the most rollouts (`topRunKey`).

### Why it is built this way

The reasons the Testing page gives, beside the definition (`src/pages/method.astro`):

- **Every place counts the same.** The score is built slot by slot, so every place on the table counts the same however many rollouts went there. In machine-learning words each share is that slot's recall and the total is the balanced accuracy.
- **The bin is left out**: it is an easy target and would lift the score.
- **No true negatives.** Every rollout asks for some slot. A false negative for s is a rollout asked for s whose vial ended anywhere else: at another slot, beside s, still in the gripper, or never picked up.
- **Not plain accuracy.** Pooling every rollout would let the slots a run was tried at most decide its score.
- **The mean, not the median.** The median hides slots that fail: seven slots at 80% and five at 0% have a median of 80% but a mean of 47%.
- **The 70% bar is on the mean, not on every slot.** Ten rollouts still leave room for luck: a slot that really works 80% of the time scores 6 of 10 or less about one time in eight, so a model that is really 80% good everywhere would show at least one slot below 7 of 10 about four times in five. The mean of 120 rollouts evens that out. (Checked for this record as binomial arithmetic: P(X ≤ 6 | n = 10, p = 0.8) = 0.121, and 1 − (1 − 0.121)^12 = 0.79.)

### Worked out on one run

### The fixed slot split and the training sets

`src/lib/score.ts` splits trained and held-out slots the same fixed way for every run. The per-destination demonstration counts in `demo_sets` of `src/data/results.json` show that each of the three training sets, `vial-sort-slots-100`, `vial-sort-slots-220` and `vial-sort-slots-220 minus 8 held out` (Isaac's), has destinations L1, L3, L6, R1, R3, R6 and the bin only, so the fixed split matches every set as recorded there. For sources that is slots 2 and 5, for destinations 2, 4 and 5, in all three sets.

## The 12-move gate

A pass test for the π0.5 retrains on the 220 demonstrations, written in the Lab note of 4 Nov and applied on 8 and 9 Nov. It is a protocol of the November sessions, not part of the score in `src/lib/score.ts`, and the website does not show it.

**Protocol** (`docs/experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md`, "After the retrain — the 12-take gate"): keep the vial layout fixed within each block and change only the destination in the command, so a model still reading the destination off the cameras cannot score: all four rollouts of a block look the same to it.

| Block | Layout | Commands |
|---|---|---|
| A: one source, three destinations | vial at R3 | `/t R3 L1`, `/t R3 L3`, `/t R3 L6`, `/t R3 bin` |
| B: the same, other rack | vial at L3 | `/t L3 R1`, `/t L3 R3`, `/t L3 R6`, `/t L3 bin` |
| C: same rack | as needed | `/t R6 R1`, `/t R1 R6`, `/t L1 L6`, `/t L6 L1` |

**Pass, "from the plan"**: destination right in at least 9 of 12, source right in at least 11 of 12. In the applications below "right slot" counts missed drops at the right slot as reaching it, which the score does not.

| Session | Placed right | Right slot, counting missed drops (pass ≥ 9) | Right vial picked (pass ≥ 11) | Verdict as written | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 Nov, Mango Lassi (B at 4000), over all 35 rollouts | 6 of 35 | not stated as a 12-move count | not stated as a 12-move count | "not met" | `docs/experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md` |
| 8–9 Nov, Mango Pudding (B at 16000) | 5 of 12 | 7 of 12 | 10 of 12 | not passed | `docs/experiments/2026-11-08-b16000-arm-test/b16000-arm-test.md` |
| 9 Nov, Plain Bagel (A at 16000) | 7 of 12 | 9 of 12 | 11 of 12 | meets it, with no margin; on strict first tries 8 of 12 right slot, one short | `docs/experiments/2026-11-09-a16000-arm-test/a16000-arm-test.md` |

Caveat written with the 8 Nov result: L1 → L6 is in none of the 220 training demonstrations; without it the gate still fails.

Isaac's notes carry a stopping rule of the same kind: after block 4, "3 or fewer of the 16 held-out (2, 4, 5) in the asked slot -> stop" (`notes/I_21000.txt`). Counted from that file for this record: 6 of the 16 held-out moves in blocks 1–4 ended in the asked slot (every slot-2 move; no slot-4 or slot-5 move), and the test went on to all eight blocks.

## The offline gate

Separate from the arm tests, the offline gate tests a saved checkpoint without the robot: it is shown recorded frames and commands and asked what motion it would predict (`src/lib/glossary.ts`). `src/data/gate.json` holds it for the 12 checkpoints of the `pi05-canon` run; [Training](../training/training.md) owns it.

## What the numbers cannot say

From the Testing page (`src/pages/method.astro`, "What the numbers cannot say") and the home page (`src/pages/index.astro`, "What is not solved"):

- **They are small.** The biggest run has 82 rollouts and many have one to five, so one more good or bad rollout moves a run a long way. No run has 10 rollouts at any rack slot, so every run is disqualified (computed for [Results](../results/results.md)).
- **Not a controlled comparison.** The runs differ in training data, length and mode.
- **Not a planned evaluation.** These were test runs made during development: 49 sessions (the distinct sessions of the rows in `src/data/experiments.json`), uneven in the moves tried, with no fixed number of rollouts per move.
- **Not comparable with August.** A person-scored evaluation of a different, earlier system (79 human-scored rollouts, `scripts/build_data.py` header) is on the site's August pages; the two should not be compared.
- **The scorer cannot see the vial.** For the 19 runs scored from the arm, "in the asked slot" means the arm let go at the asked slot within half a slot. Of the 53 such rollouts a person watched, 39 had the vial outside the slot (`eye` in `src/data/results.json`).
- **The notes are one person's reading at the robot**, turned into outcomes by the regular expressions above; a misreading is fixed by hand in the yaml (`scripts/notes_to_yaml.py`).

## Where sources disagree

- **Sweet Corn's rate.** The Testing page says FLUX "ran at 15.5 a second in the one session whose rate was logged". `src/data/experiments.json` also gives a rate for Sweet Corn on 7 October: 15.3 Hz. Both are FLUX; Sweet Corn is not scored on the site.
- **What counts as reaching the slot.** The 12-move gate of the Lab notes counts a missed drop at the right slot as "right slot"; the score, and every stage count since "the operator's rule, 24 Nov" (`scripts/build_data.py`), counts it as not in the asked slot.
- **Rollout counts of 4 Nov.** The Lab note counts 35 rollouts for B at 4000 (6 full successes, 14 failed at the grasp), 10 for `004000_q37` and 4 for `004000_q37g`. The data, scored from the arm, has 37 for Mango Lassi (6 in the asked slot, 16 never picked it up), 11 for Mango Sorbet and 7 for Mango Chutney (`src/data/results.json`). The Lab note scores from the operator's words; the data from the scorer, which also counts takes the note numbers once. The difference was not traced.
