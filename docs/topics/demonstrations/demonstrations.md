# Demonstrations

## Scope

This record owns the demonstration data of the current rig: the 100-demonstration set and the 220-demonstration set, what each is, when and how it was recorded, its size, why the second was recorded the way it was, how the commands are worded, which moves and slots it covers and which are held out, its recording batches, the camera differences between batches, its known problems, the copies the models trained on, and where it is published.

It does not own the rig itself (the arm, the racks and slot names, the cameras and which model sees which, what a move is): that is the [task and rig record](../task-and-rig/task-and-rig.md). It does not own training runs, rollouts or scores: those are in the [Lab notes](../../experiments/experiments.md) and on the website's pages. How the site turns data into pages is the [website record](../website/website.md).

## What a demonstration is

One move recorded by a person driving the leader arm while the follower arm copied it, with all cameras and joint positions logged (`src/lib/glossary.ts`). Each demonstration is one episode of a LeRobot dataset (format `v3.0`, `src/data/datasets.json`). All cameras record at 30 frames a second (`src/pages/datasets.astro`).

## The 100 demonstrations

Recorded on 14 October 2026. From `src/pages/datasets.astro` ("How it was recorded"):

- **Twenty runs of five.** Each run in one sitting, with one destination per run.
- **Always from the other rack.** For a rack destination the vial always starts in the other rack; no move stays inside a rack. 75 go from one rack to the other and 25 to the bin.
- **Colour never identifies the vial.** Three vial colours, red, cyan and dark green. In 95 of the 100 scenes a second vial of the target's colour stands on the source rack; the 5 exceptions are the first run.
- **The bin moves.** To a new spot every ten demonstrations, with small nudges in between. The first frame of every demonstration is saved so each spot is on record.
- **The light fades.** The room got darker through the afternoon. The side cameras are almost black in demonstrations 75 to 99; the overhead camera stays readable.
- **The operator is in view.** The arm is homed before every demonstration, and at the start the operator leans over the robot, so the person is in the wrist and across-the-table views.
- **Three recorded again.** The wrist camera dropped off the bus three times (demonstrations 47, 53 and 60). Those three were recorded again.

The five camera stills on the Data page and the five-camera video are from this set: `scripts/make_cams.py` takes one frame of episode 10 (by default at 55% of the episode) from `data/vial-sort-ws100`; `curation.yaml` (`ws100_demo`) and `src/data/media.json` (`demo_ws100`) use episode 10, 22.8 s.

## Why the 220 were recorded the way they were

In the 100, every run of five had one destination and was recorded in one sitting, so the camera picture said which sitting a frame was from, the sitting said the destination, and the models learned to read the destination off the picture rather than the command. In the 150 new demonstrations one layout is recorded three times in a row with three different destinations, so only the command can tell them apart (`src/pages/datasets.astro`, "The 220, so only the command names the destination"; `src/pages/index.astro`, chapter 02).

The Lab notes call these "contrastive" demonstrations and the sheet they were recorded from `ee/SORT_SHEET_WS_CONTRAST.txt` ([Arm test, 4 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md)). The same note's test protocol keeps the vial layout fixed within a block of rollouts and changes only the destination in the command, "the whole point of the contrastive data".

The 220 still contain the first 70 of the 100, which were recorded one destination per run.

## How the commands are worded

A command names the source by slot and rack and the destination by slot and rack or the bin, never by colour (`src/pages/datasets.astro`). Each part is drawn from its own pool. The pools are recorded in `src/data/october.json`, imported by `scripts/build_data.py` from the module `relabel_positions` on the workstation, not retyped. The Data page (`src/pages/datasets.astro`) shows each pool with its values and multiplies their sizes into the number of possible wordings of one move.

| What | Where it is |
|---|---|
| Verbs, objects, bin names and sentence forms | `coverage.pools.verb`, `.object`, `.bin`, `.template` |
| The forms of each slot | `coverage.pools.slot`, one list per slot number; `coverage.pools.slot_count` is the number of forms per slot |
| The forms of each rack | `coverage.pools.rack.left` and `.right` |
| What was recorded for the 100: distinct wordings, moves, most wordings of one move | `coverage.wordings.distinct`, `.moves`, `.max_per_move` |
| One move's recorded wordings, as an example | `coverage.wordings.example_move` and `.example` |

How a wording was chosen: not at random. The k-th demonstration of a move gets the k-th combination, so repeats are phrased differently (`src/pages/datasets.astro`). When: written once, afterwards. A run was recorded with its destination only; one script then read the recording sheet and rewrote every command. No video was touched (`src/pages/datasets.astro`). The pool sizes, the possible wordings they multiply to and the counts of recorded wordings are under [readings](#readings-at-commit-8065309).

The copies the models trained on replace these with one sentence per move: Mango Rice's data is "one canonical sentence per move", and every model on the 220 has "one sentence per move" (`src/data/registry.json`). `scripts/build_ws220.py` reads the moves of the 220 from prompts of the form "position N of the left/right rack to slot N of the left/right rack" or "… to the bin" in `vial-sort-ws220_canon`. How the published `vial-sort-slots-220` words its commands is not stated in this repository.

## Coverage move by move

How many demonstrations each move has is in the data, one entry per move:

| What | The 100 | The 220 |
|---|---|---|
| Demonstrations of each move | `coverage.demos` in `src/data/october.json` | `demos` in `src/data/ws220.json` |
| Demonstrations per source slot and per destination | `coverage.per_source`, `coverage.per_destination` | `per_source`, `per_destination` |
| Different moves, moves inside one rack, moves to the bin | counted from `coverage.demos` | `moves`, `same_rack`, `to_bin` |
| Rack slots, held-out sources and destinations | `coverage.slots`, `coverage.heldout_sources`, `coverage.heldout_destinations` | `slots`, `heldout_sources`, `heldout_destinations` |

A key of `demos` is a move written `source->destination`, for example `L1->R3` or `R4->bin`; its value is the number of demonstrations of that move. A move without a key has no demonstration. The Data page (`src/pages/datasets.astro`) draws both as matrices, source by destination, with `src/components/CoverageMatrix.astro`; the slots 2 and 5 as sources and 2, 4 and 5 as destinations are held out in both sets.

The counts the record argues from (moves, moves inside one rack, moves to the bin, and the gaps L1 → L6 and L4 → L3 in the 220) are under [readings](#readings-at-commit-8065309). The 100 have no move inside a rack (`src/pages/datasets.astro`). The 220 have one gap the Data page names, L1 → L6, and one the page does not name, L4 → L3 (see [where the sources disagree](#where-the-sources-disagree)).

The same-rack moves come only from the 150 new demonstrations, since the 100 have none; the 4 Nov Lab note gives "~50 of the 150 episodes" ([Arm test, 4 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md)). The split of moves by batch is not in `src/data/ws220.json`.

### Held out

Both sets hold out the same slots: slots 2 and 5 of either rack are never a source, and slots 2, 4 and 5 of either rack are never a destination (`heldout_sources`, `heldout_destinations` in `src/data/october.json` and `src/data/ws220.json`; set by rule in the scripts, see [above](#where-these-facts-come-from)). Slot 4 is only ever a source. The glossary's definition: never recorded on purpose; they test whether a model can reach slots no demonstration used (`src/lib/glossary.ts`). The Lab notes test these slots on the arm (for example `R3 → L5`, `L3 → R4`, `R5 → L1`, `L2 → R6`); see [Arm test, 8–9 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-08-b16000-arm-test/b16000-arm-test.md) and [Arm test, 9 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-09-a16000-arm-test/a16000-arm-test.md).

## Known problems

- **The destination is in the picture (the 100).** One destination per run, so the scene names the destination; the reason the 220 were recorded (see [above](#why-the-220-were-recorded-the-way-they-were)).
- **Dark recordings.** The side cameras are almost black in demonstrations 75 to 99 of the 100; runs 15 to 20 were dropped from the 220.
- **Operator in view at the start.** The operator leans over the robot at the start of each demonstration, in the wrist and across-the-table views. The training copies replace those start frames with views of the arm with nobody near it ("operator-free start", `src/lib/glossary.ts`; `src/data/registry.json`).
- **Wrist camera dropped off the bus** three times in the 100 (demonstrations 47, 53 and 60); those three were recorded again.
- **`left_top` moved** between the 100 and the later batches, and again after all recordings, on 2026-11-04.
- **No L1 → L6 (and no L4 → L3).** The page names L1 → L6 as the one same-rack gap in the 220; the data also has no L4 → L3. The 12-move gate used L1 → L6 anyway ([Arm test, 8–9 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-08-b16000-arm-test/b16000-arm-test.md): "L1→L6 is in none of the 220 training episodes").
- **Gripper range widened by the merge.** LeRobot's merge does not recompute percentiles but takes a min/max envelope over per-episode statistics. 15 of the 220 episodes (9 from the 28 Oct set, 6 from the depth set) open the gripper past 37.4°, up to 50.9°, and set the gripper's normalisation span to 50.8° for all 220; the true 99th percentile over the merged frames is 36.5°. The fix (pinning the percentiles in the dataset) belongs to training ([Arm test, 4 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md), [Retraining on the cluster](../../experiments/2026-11-07-retrains-on-the-cluster/retrains-on-the-cluster.md)).
- **A label correction of episode 141** is named, without detail, as "not the cause" in [Arm test, 4 Nov](../../experiments/2026-11-04-b4000-arm-test/b4000-arm-test.md). What was corrected is not stated in this repository.

## Readings at commit 8065309

These readings were counted from `src/data/october.json`, `src/data/ws220.json` and `src/data/datasets.json` at commit 8065309 (2026-11-28) and rechecked against the same files on 2026-11-28 by a one-off script outside the repository; the data under `src/data/` has not changed since that commit. They rerun nothing and change no conclusion. When the data is rebuilt, check them again before quoting them as current.

- **The 100** (`coverage`): 100 demonstrations, 58,650 frames at 30 a second, 33 minutes; 32 different moves, 75 from one rack to the other, 25 to the bin, none inside a rack. Each source slot has 11 to 13 demonstrations.
- **The 220**: 220 demonstrations, 141,735 frames at 30 a second, 79 minutes; 48 different moves (`moves`), 41 to the bin (`to_bin`), 51 inside one rack (`same_rack`), so 128 from one rack to the other (arithmetic for this record: 220 − 41 − 51).
- **Gaps in the 220.** No demonstration goes from L1 to L6, and none from L4 to L3; these are the only two moves between trained slots without a demonstration. R4 has all three same-rack destinations.
- **Batches of the 220** (`batches`): `ws100` 70 demonstrations and 44,322 frames, `contrast` 51 and 36,420 (2026-10-28), `contrast+depth` 99 and 60,993 (2026-11-03). They add up to `episodes` and `frames` (arithmetic for this record: 70 + 51 + 99 = 220; 44,322 + 36,420 + 60,993 = 141,735). The 30 demonstrations of the 100 not in the 220 are 14,328 frames (arithmetic for this record: 58,650 − 44,322).
- **Wordings of the 100** (`coverage.wordings`): 100 distinct wordings across 32 moves, one per demonstration, at most 4 for one move; the example move, `R3->L6`, has three.
- **Pool sizes** (`coverage.pools`): 6 verbs, 4 objects, 5 forms per slot, 4 per rack, 5 for the bin, 4 sentence forms. Slot 6 has "the last slot" in place of "spot 6". The Data page multiplies these: 6 × 4 × (5 × 4) × (5 × 4) × 4 = 38,400 wordings of one rack-to-rack move, and 6 × 4 × (5 × 4) × 5 × 4 = 9,600 of one move to the bin (computed in `src/pages/datasets.astro`; design arithmetic, not a count of recorded wordings).
- **Published sets** (`datasets`): `vial-sort-slots-100` uploaded 2026-10-14, 100 episodes, 58,650 frames; `vial-sort-slots-220`, `vial-sort-contrast-51` and `vial-sort-contrast-99-depth` uploaded 2026-11-10, with 220, 51 and 99 episodes and 141,735, 36,420 and 60,993 frames. Only `vial-sort-contrast-99-depth` carries `rs_depth`.
- **Earlier sets** (`datasets`): six, uploaded 2026-06-07 to 2026-08-22, from 40 to 150 episodes; no name with version 3.

## Where the sources disagree

- **Which dark runs.** The batch table says runs 15 to 20 were dropped as too dark (30 demonstrations, matching 100 − 70); the recording notes say the side cameras are almost black in demonstrations 75 to 99 (25 demonstrations). Both in `src/pages/datasets.astro`; how runs and demonstrations are numbered is not stated.
- **"One layout recorded three times" for the whole 220.** The 220's row on the Data page and the hero (`src/components/Hero.astro`: "recorded so the destination can only come from the command") describe all 220 this way, while 70 of them are from the 100, recorded one destination per run (`src/pages/datasets.astro`, batch table).
- **Gaps in the 220.** `src/pages/datasets.astro` says "One gap: no demonstration goes from L1 to L6". `src/data/ws220.json` also has no `L4->L3`, a same-rack move from a trained source to a trained destination. Whether that one was meant to be left out is not stated.
- **Batch names.** The same batches are `ws100` / `contrast` / `contrast+depth` in `src/data/ws220.json`, "the first 70" / "first sitting" / "second sitting" on the Data page, and "the old 70" / "batch 1" / "batch 2" in the 9 Nov Lab note.
- **"Held out" has two meanings.** In `src/lib/glossary.ts` it means slots never recorded on purpose; in `src/data/registry.json` and `src/data/results.json`, "minus 8 held out" means 8 demonstrations removed from Isaac 0.5 Expert's training copy.
- **Gripper maximum.** The 4 Nov Lab note gives the merged normalisation span as 50.8° and the widest episodes as "up to 50.9°"; it does not say whether these are the same quantity.
- **The third vial colour** is "dark green" on the Data page, "lime" in the site's drawings and `curation.yaml`, "green" on the August page.
